Frequently Asked Questions

    
How do I take my measurements at home?
All you need is a standard measuring tape. Wear thin, well-fitting clothes and measure around the fullest part of your bust, natural waist, and hips. Our smart engine adds the proper comfort ease for your chosen fit.

    
Can I request pockets in any dress?
Yes! Simply choose "Hidden Side Pockets" in the customization options. We ensure pockets are deep enough for your smartphone.

    
What if the dress needs a minor adjustment?
Every dress is tailored with a 1.5-inch inner alteration margin. If any adjustment is needed upon delivery, message us on WhatsApp and we will happily take care of it for you.
