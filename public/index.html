Questions

    
How do I know my size?
Each measurement has a short guide, and the stitch size is worked out for you with the right room added for your fit. If you would rather not measure yourself, choose "Call me and guide me" and we will help you over the phone.

    
How long does stitching take?
Most orders are stitched in  once your measurements are confirmed. Delivery time depends on your city.

    
What if it does not fit?
Message us after you try it on. If the fit is off, we will arrange an alteration.

    
How do I pay?
After you send your order, we confirm the final price on WhatsApp and share how to pay.
